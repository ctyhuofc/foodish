FoodishBot

Foodish lists restaurant deals in Edmonton, Vancouver and Toronto: wing nights, happy hours and other specials. FoodishBot is the small robot that finds them by reading each restaurant's own website.

What it does

  • It runs once a day, early in the morning (about 4 am Mountain time).
  • It reads a restaurant's home page and up to four linked pages that look like specials, deals or happy hour. It never reads more than five pages per site.
  • It waits between pages, and it follows each site's robots.txt.
  • It identifies itself as FoodishBot/1.0 with a link to this page.
  • An AI model picks out the deals from the page text. Foodish only lists a deal if its wording and price appear on your site, and every listing links back to the page it came from.

The list of restaurants comes from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps. Foodish doesn't sell anything, and it doesn't take payment for listings.

Is your listing wrong?

Email yates@foodish.ca with your restaurant's name and address and what needs fixing. If a deal has ended, updating your own website fixes Foodish too: the robot rereads your site the next morning.

Want to be removed?

Email the same address. Your restaurant will be taken off Foodish, and the robot will stop visiting your site.

You can also block the robot yourself. Add these lines to your site's robots.txt:

User-agent: FoodishBot
Disallow: /

Its deals come off Foodish at the next morning's run.

Privacy

Foodish has no accounts, no cookies and no ads. It doesn't know who you are.

Your location

If you allow it, your phone tells the app where you are so it can sort deals by distance. That happens on your phone. Your location isn't sent to Foodish unless you turn on alerts (see below). The map is centred on where you are, so OpenStreetMap, which draws it, sees your IP address and the part of the map you're looking at (roughly your neighbourhood).

What's saved on your phone

Your settings are saved in your browser so they're there next time: your city, distance, light or dark look, the last place you were, and your alert choices. Clearing your browser's data for foodish.ca removes them.

Counting visits

Foodish uses GoatCounter to count visits, so we can tell whether anyone uses it. It doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't keep your IP address or follow you to other sites. It records which page was viewed (not what you searched for), the site you came from, your browser type, screen size and rough country.

Alerts (only if you turn them on)

For "deals near you today" notifications, Foodish stores what it needs to send them:

  • your browser's notification address
  • your location, rounded to about 100 metres
  • your distance and the kinds of deals you picked

It's kept with Cloudflare, which runs the alerts service. Turning alerts off deletes it (if the service can't be reached right then, the app tries again each time you open Foodish). If you block notifications in your browser settings or clear Foodish's data instead, it's deleted the next time an alert to you fails to arrive. To have it deleted sooner, email yates@foodish.ca.

Telling us about a deal

The "A deal we missed?" form opens your own email app with the restaurant, the deal and any link filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send there, and your message then reaches us like any email, from your email address.

Other services the app uses

Like any website, these receive your IP address when your browser contacts them:

  • GitHub, which hosts the site and the daily deal files
  • Google Fonts, for the lettering
  • OpenStreetMap, for the map pictures (when you open the map; on a wide screen the map is always showing)
